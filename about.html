Our story

From Hawler to Edgware Road

A Kurdish & Iraqi grill house built on charcoal fire, generous plates, and the welcome guests talk about long after the meal.

Heritage

Cooking that tastes of home

Hawler — also known as Erbil — is one of the world’s oldest continuously inhabited cities, and the capital of the Kurdistan Region. Our kitchen carries that name to London with recipes built around charcoal, slow braises, and fresh Slemani naan.

On Edgware Road we sit among London’s best-known Middle Eastern strip — but guests tell us what sets Hawler apart is simpler: you are treated as family before the first skewer lands.
