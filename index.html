Our Culinary Heritage

Bringing the Heart of Kurdistan to Edgware Road

Named after Hawler (Erbil), the historic capital of the Kurdistan Region, Hawler Restaurant was established with a singular mission: to serve genuine, comforting, and authentic Middle Eastern cuisine in London.

Every day, our master chefs prepare tender cuts of lamb and chicken, hand-marinated in traditional spices and grilled over real charcoal. From our famous Kuzi Lamb Shanks simmering in rich spiced broths to hot flatbread baked continuously in our traditional clay oven, every bite is a celebration of flavor.
