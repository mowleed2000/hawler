The Hawler welcome

Before you order, you are already a guest

Named after Hawler — Erbil, the historic capital of the Kurdistan Region — we cook the way grill houses do back home, and greet you the same way.

  • 01

    Complimentary starters

    House soup, salad, hummus and hot Slemani naan arrive on the house — the tradition guests mention again and again.

  • 02

    Real charcoal fire

    Lamb and chicken skewers, chops and sea bass take the smoke of an open charcoal grill — not a flat plate.

  • 03

    Tea to close the meal

    Kurdish tea — and often baklava — finishes the evening. Generous plates, family tables, honest prices.
