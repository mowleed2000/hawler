The Hawler welcome

Before you order, you are already a guest

Named after Hawler (Erbil), the ancient capital of Kurdistan, we cook the way homes and grill houses do back home — and we greet you the same way.

  • 01

    Complimentary starters

    House soup, salad, hummus and hot Slemani naan arrive at the table on the house — the tradition guests mention in nearly every review.

  • 02

    Real charcoal, not a grill plate

    Lamb and chicken skewers, chops and sea bass take the smoke of an open charcoal fire — the flavour Edgware Road is known for.

  • 03

    Tea to close the meal

    Kurdish tea — and often baklava — finishes the evening. Generous plates, family tables, honest prices.
